Mass-transport limits in a microfluidic GDE electrolyser

Communications Chemistry · 2024

Gas diffusion electrodes deliver CO2 to the catalyst in the gas phase, but performance is still set by how fast reactants and products move through the porous layers and electrolyte.

Key findings

  • Validated 2D model of the cathode side of a microfluidic CO2-to-CO flow cell with a gas diffusion electrode.
  • With a fully flooded catalyst layer the CO partial current density peaks at 75 mA cm−2 at −1.3 V vs RHE, then falls as CO2 near the catalyst runs out.
  • A large part of the catalyst layer stays underutilised; graded porosity and anisotropic layers are proposed to improve mass transport.
  • Higher CO2 gas flow raises the CO current but lowers CO2 conversion efficiency: a clear trade-off.

What I did

  • Two-dimensional model of a gas-diffusion cathode in a microfluidic flow cell, resolving coupled charge, species and momentum transport.
  • Validated against experimental data.
  • Quantified which transport processes limit performance and where.
  • Interactive viewer and movie below: the model across the full potential sweep.
